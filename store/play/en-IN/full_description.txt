Brandhoop Mobile Point is the shop on Abhay Colony, Neem Ka Thana, across the road from Study Point Library. This is its own app. Browse the same phones and accessories you see at the counter, reserve one from home, and walk in whenever you like to pick it up.

BUY OR RESERVE FROM ANYWHERE
• Phones, smartwatches, earbuds, chargers, cases, screen guards and more, with clear prices and offers
• Reserve a phone with a small token amount and collect it at the shop
• Home delivery to nearby pincodes, or pick up at the store
• Track every order and keep your invoices in one place

PAY THE WAY YOU PREFER
• UPI, debit and credit cards, netbanking
• Cash at the store, or cash on delivery where available
• Bajaj Finserv EMI is available only at the shop, because it needs a face scan; the lender decides on approval

TRUST OF A LOCAL SHOP
• Real shop address, opening hours and one-tap directions
• Plan a visit and see what to bring with you
• Exchange estimate for your old phone
• Book a repair and follow its status
• Warranty and IMEI details with every phone

HINDI AND ENGLISH
Switch between Hindi and English at any time. The whole app, including prices and dates, follows your choice.

FOR THE SHOP TEAM
Staff, managers and the owner sign in to the same app and see a counter, stock, orders, EMI desk, repairs and reports that match their role. Customers never see these screens.

Note: your sign-in is your mobile number and a one-time code. Prices, offers and stock are set by the shop and can change.
