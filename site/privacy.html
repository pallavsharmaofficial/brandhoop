Privacy

Last updated 28 October 2026

The short version

Brandhoop has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. We do not collect your name, number or location, and the app does not send anything to a server of ours.

When you tap “Send order on WhatsApp”, the message you see is handed to WhatsApp, and you decide whether to send it. After that, WhatsApp’s own policy applies.

Who is responsible

Brandhoop is the app of Brandhoop Mobile Point, Abhay Colony, Neem Ka Thana, Rajasthan 332713. Questions about this policy, and requests about your information, go to the project’s issue tracker or to the shop at its address above.

What stays on your device

The app saves, on your phone only: your language and theme choice, the items in your cart, and a copy of the shop’s catalogue and offers so it opens quickly without internet. Clearing the app’s storage in your phone’s settings, or uninstalling, removes all of it.

Permissions

The app declares one permission, internet access, which it uses only to open links such as Google Maps directions, a phone call or WhatsApp when you tap them. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

The network

Each time it opens, the app downloads one public file from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com, with a copy on this site) that holds the shop’s products, prices and offers, and it loads the shop’s photos from there too. The request carries no information about you beyond what any web request carries, such as your IP address, which GitHub, who hosts this site, can see. The web version is also hosted on GitHub Pages. Links you tap, such as directions, a call or WhatsApp, open another app or your browser, and that app’s own policy applies.

Other people's code

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code. It uses only open Flutter packages for state, navigation, translation and local storage.

Children

The app is a shop app for adults and is not directed at children under 13. Buying on EMI or by card needs an adult.

Keeping and deleting your information

Data stays on your phone until you remove it. You can clear it by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app. Because nothing is sent elsewhere, there is nothing for us to delete on a server. If a shop server is added later, this section will describe how long it keeps data and how to ask for deletion.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page before it takes effect, and the date above changes with it. The source of the app is public at github.com/pallavsharmaofficial/brandhoop, so you can check what it does.
