Privacy

Last updated 28 October 2026

The short version

Brandhoop has no server of its own, no analytics and no advertising. Everything you enter stays on your phone and is not sent to the shop or to anyone else.

Before online payments are switched on this will change, and this page will change first. When the shop connects a real payment provider and a shop server, orders and your contact details will be shared with them so the shop can serve you, and this policy will say exactly what.

What stays on your device

The app saves the following on your phone, using the app’s private storage: your chosen language, your name and mobile number, saved delivery addresses, your cart and wishlist, your orders and invoices, EMI plans and Autopay settings, repair and exchange requests, and the shop’s catalogue. In the current version the sample data you see is made up and generated on the phone. Card numbers and UPI IDs you type while paying are checked on the phone and are not saved.

Permissions

The app declares one permission, internet access, which it uses only to open links such as Google Maps directions, a phone call or WhatsApp when you tap them. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

The network

The app itself makes no network request. When you tap a link such as directions, a call or WhatsApp, your phone opens another app or the browser, and that app’s own privacy policy applies.

Other people's code

The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting code. It uses only open Flutter packages for state, navigation, translation and local storage.

Children

The app is a shop app for adults and is not directed at children under 13. Buying on EMI or by card needs an adult.

Keeping and deleting your information

Data stays on your phone until you remove it. You can clear it by clearing the app’s storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app. Because nothing is sent elsewhere, there is nothing for us to delete on a server. If a shop server is added later, this section will describe how long it keeps data and how to ask for deletion.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page before it takes effect, and the date above changes with it. The source of the app is public at github.com/pallavsharmaofficial/brandhoop, so you can check what it does.
