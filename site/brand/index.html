Using the logo

  • Use the full-colour logo on light grey or white, the “on dark” logo on navy or photos with a dark overlay.
  • Use the one-colour or white version when printing in a single colour, on stamps, or on bills.
  • Leave clear space around the logo at least the height of the “B” bowl. Do not stretch, recolour or add shadows.
  • The business card, poster, board and letterhead carry the shop address and phone. If the address or phone changes, ask Claude to regenerate them, or use the Design Studio in the admin panel.
