# Getting customers to the app

The app only grows the shop if people install it and use it. These are
low-cost steps for a town shop. Do the first three before anything paid.

## Make the app the easy way to buy
1. **QR at the counter and on the bill.** A printed QR to the Play listing; staff
   say one line: "install and your warranty and EMI are in one place".
2. **Every walk-in is registered** through the POS, so the customer already has a
   profile when they install.
3. **WhatsApp.** Set the shop's WhatsApp number in Store settings. Share product
   and offer links from the app to the shop's status and customer groups.

## Be found
- **Google Business Profile** for Brandhoop Mobile Point: real photos, hours, the
  phone number, "across the road from Study Point Library", posts for offers.
  Ask each happy buyer for a review.
- Link the Play listing and the website (`site/`) from the profile.
- Put the shop name, address and QR on the board and the counter.

## Reach beyond the town
- Short videos of each new phone on the shop's Instagram and YouTube Shorts, in Hindi.
- Say what makes the shop different: you can reserve for ₹1,000 and see it before
  you pay, EMI with Bajaj Finance, repair in the same place.
- Festival offers (Diwali, Teej, wedding season) with a coupon in the app.
- Referral code: the app has one; give a real reward and say it out loud at billing.

## Keep them
- EMI due reminders and order updates (push, once the backend exists).
- A quick follow-up after delivery. Ask for the review then.
- Repairs are the best reason to return. Promote the free quote.

## Measure it
Use the dashboard scorecard (`docs/GROWTH_PLAN.md`): installs are not the goal;
phones sold, attach rate, repeat customers and repairs are.

## Cautions
- Advertise EMI only with the lender's approved wording. Do not say "no
  documents" or "guaranteed approval".
- Do not use customers' photos or numbers without their consent.
